What types of tires and auto repair services do you offer?

As a full service tire shop, we provide comprehensive auto maintenance including suspension checks, brake service, oil changes and tire installation. We carry top brands like Michelin and Goodyear, from all-season tires to dedicated winter sets. We also handle complex diagnostics and check engine light troubleshooting for personal vehicles and commercial fleets.

How can I schedule an appointment for service or new tires?

Request a quote through this site, call us directly to discuss your vehicle, or walk into our Laramie location. Our early-bird hours starting at seven in the morning make it easy to drop off before work.

What areas do you serve, and what are your hours?

We serve drivers across Laramie, Harmony, Buford, Pine Bluffs and surrounding rural Wyoming communities. We are open Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Saturdays 7:00 AM to noon, and closed Sundays.

Do you offer manufacturer rebates or promotions?

Yes. We frequently have manufacturer rebates and coupons running, and we will check every current offer against your quote. See the current rebates or ask at the counter.

What makes your auto center different from other local mechanics?

With over twenty-five years in business, our family-owned center is built on honest, transparent service. Troy, Sue and the team offer a collective forty-five years of industry experience. Customers trust us because we accurately diagnose complex issues that others miss, and because the price we quote is the price you pay.

How often should I get an alignment in Wyoming?

Once a year is a reasonable baseline here, and always with a new set of tires. Our roads and freeze-thaw cycles knock vehicles out of spec faster than in milder climates. If the car pulls, the wheel sits off-center, or your tread is wearing unevenly, have it checked sooner.
